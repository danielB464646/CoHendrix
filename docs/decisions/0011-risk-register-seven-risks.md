# ADR 0011: The risk register has 7 risks, not 10

## Context

The review deck printed three risks and said "Full register of 10 risks with owners in ARD chapter 5". The ARD kickoff note said not to invent the other seven.

## Options

1. Fill the register to 10 risks.
2. List only the risks the team can justify from the review, proposal v2 and the scope changes in the ARD.

## Decision

The ARD register (Table 5) has 7 risks: R1, R2 and R6 from the review (R2 reworded), R4, R5 and R7 from proposal v2, and R3 added because of stated constraints (ADR 0005). Owners are still TBD.

## Why

Every risk has a real source and a check date, mitigation and fallback. Adding risks only to reach 10 would invent content, which the repo rules forbid.
