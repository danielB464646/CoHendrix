# ADR 0003: The customer is DJ LIRANO

Supersedes the customer line of [ADR 0001](0001-v1-scope-after-requirements-review.md) ("No customer name is invented. The named customer is still an open question.").

## Context

The review deck filed in the repo prints "Customer [name, organization]", and ADR 0001 left the customer open so that no name would be invented. The ARD needs a named customer who decides features, priorities and acceptance.

## Options

1. Keep the placeholder until a source is filed.
2. Name the customer the team has confirmed.

## Decision

The customer is DJ LIRANO (Liron Goldvaser), a DJ and producer. The ARD names them on its title block and in Table 2 (§1.4).

## Why

The team confirmed the customer on 15 Nov 2026, so the name is no longer invented. The filed review deck still prints the placeholder; this ADR is the source for the name until an updated deck is filed.
